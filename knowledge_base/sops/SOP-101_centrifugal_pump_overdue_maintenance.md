# SOP-101: Centrifugal Pump Overdue Maintenance & Mechanical Seal Protocol
**Document ID:** SOP-101-REV4  
**Applicability:** All Centrifugal Pumps, Slurry Transfer Loops, High-Pressure Feed Drives  

## 1. Operating Principle & Risk Context
When centrifugal pumps exceed 90 days past their scheduled maintenance interval, mechanical seal degradation increases exponentially due to particulate accumulation and gland packing dry-out. Overdue maintenance combined with elevated vibration (>4.5 mm/s) frequently precedes total shaft lockup or high-pressure seal weeping.

## 2. Immediate Procedural Actions
If an asset is flagged for overdue maintenance or elevated vibration:
1. **Visual & Acoustic Audit:** Inspect primary mechanical seal packing for weeping or hydrocarbon misting. Check bearing housing temperature using handheld thermal imaging.
2. **Cooling Flush Verification:** Ensure API Plan 11/53 seal flush cooling loops are operational. Flush pressure must exceed pump stuffing box pressure by at least 1.5 bar.
3. **Lubrication Flushing:** Drain existing ISO VG 68 synthetic lube oil from the bearing reservoir. Inspect for metal particle contamination or emulsification. Refill to oil sight glass midpoint.
4. **Emergency Standby Transfer:** If shaft vibration exceeds 7.1 mm/s or bearing temperature exceeds 95°C, initiate transfer to standby pump PUMP-408A within 45 minutes and isolate suction/discharge valves.
