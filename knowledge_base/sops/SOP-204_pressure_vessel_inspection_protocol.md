# SOP-204: High-Pressure Vessel Inspection & Wall Thinning Protocol
**Document ID:** SOP-204-REV2  
**Applicability:** Distillation Reboilers, Hydrocracker Drums, Pressure Columns  

## 1. Safety Audit & Wall Thickness Verification
Industrial pressure vessels operating above 15 bar require non-destructive testing (NDT) ultrasonic thickness gauging every 180 operating days. High-severity inspection findings (corrosion pitting > 1.5 mm or wall thinning > 10% nominal thickness) demand immediate de-rate or shutdown.

## 2. Response Procedures for High Severity Findings
If physical inspection severity is rated 'HIGH' or 'CRITICAL':
1. **Operating Pressure De-rate:** Immediately reduce internal operating pressure by 20% below maximum allowable working pressure (MAWP).
2. **Ultrasonic Grid Mapping:** Perform 100-point NDT grid scanning over affected shell welds and lower skirt regions.
3. **Relief Valve Verification:** Verify secondary safety relief valves (PSV-304) pop setpoint at 110% MAWP. Conduct manual lift lever test if safe.
4. **MOC Escalation:** Issue formal Management of Change (MOC) work order for weld overlay cladding or shell section replacement before next plant turnaround.
