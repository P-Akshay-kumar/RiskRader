# SOP-712: Gas Compressor Rotor Balancing & Lube Oil Flush
**Document ID:** SOP-712-REV4  
**Applicability:** Turbine Compressors, Centrifugal Air Compressors  

## 1. Operating Context
Gas compressors experiencing repeated emergency failure repairs or high vibration (> 6.0 mm/s) require dynamic rotor balancing and bearing clearance inspection.

## 2. Dynamic Balancing & Flush Protocol
1. **Lube Oil Flushing:** Flush ISO VG 46 turbine oil through 5-micron absolute filter elements for 4 hours until ISO 4406 cleanliness count reaches 15/13/10.
2. **Rotor Runout Inspection:** Measure shaft dial indicator runout. Max allowable total indicator reading (TIR) is 0.012 mm.
3. **Multi-Plane Dynamic Balance:** Balance rotor on dynamic balancing rig to ISO 1940 Grade G2.5 precision.
