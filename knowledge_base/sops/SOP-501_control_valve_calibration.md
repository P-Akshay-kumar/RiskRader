# SOP-501: Control Valve Calibration & Stem Packing Binding
**Document ID:** SOP-501-REV3  
**Applicability:** Inlet Valves, Flare Bypass Valves, Pressure Regulators  

## 1. Operating Anomalies & Stem Packing Friction
Control valves suffering from excessive stem packing friction or actuator air leakage present high risk of sticking during emergency safety shutoff events. Sticking control valves are often indicated by telemetry hysteresis > 5% or zero positioner travel feedback.

## 2. Maintenance & Calibration Procedure
1. **Pneumatic Actuator Leak Test:** Apply 3.5 bar shop air to actuator diaphragm; inspect for pressure drop using soap bubble solution.
2. **Packing Nut Torque:** Tighten PTFE chevron packing gland nuts to 18 N·m using calibrated torque wrench. Do not over-torque to prevent stem binding.
3. **Smart Positioner Auto-Stroke:** Execute 5-point calibration loop (0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% stroke). Verify linear feedback response.
4. **Emergency Trip Test:** Perform full partial-stroke test (PST) to confirm 100% fail-safe closure within 3.0 seconds.
