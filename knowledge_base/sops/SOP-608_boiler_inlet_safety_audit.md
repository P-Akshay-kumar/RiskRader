# SOP-608: Auxiliary Boiler Inlet Safety & Steam Pressure Relief Audit
**Document ID:** SOP-608-REV2  
**Applicability:** Auxiliary Boilers, Steam Headers, Inlet Manifolds  

## 1. Safety Audit Guidelines
Auxiliary boiler inlet systems require mandatory safety audits every 90 days. High incident history or overdue inspections increase risk of thermal shock cracking in steam drum inlet headers.

## 2. Emergency Operational Steps
1. **Safety Valve Popping Test:** Test primary steam relief valves (PSV-608) at 105% operating pressure. Ensure clean seat reset without simmering.
2. **Water Column Blowdown:** Flush water column and low-water fuel cutoff float chamber to clear sludge accumulation.
3. **Draft Fan Inspection:** Inspect forced draft fan bearing vibration and air damper actuator stroke travel.
