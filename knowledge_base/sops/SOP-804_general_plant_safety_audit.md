# SOP-804: General Plant Hazard Escalation & Emergency Isolation Protocol
**Document ID:** SOP-804-REV1  
**Applicability:** All Refinery & Chemical Plant Assets  

## 1. Hazard Escalation Principles
When any critical asset exhibits combined risk factors (e.g. overdue maintenance > 180 days AND high inspection severity findings), emergency isolation protocols must be initiated within 2 hours.

## 2. Emergency Escalation Workflow
1. **Control Room Alarm Escalation:** Notify Lead Board Operator and Shift Supervisor of dual-engine risk alert.
2. **Field Safety Barrier Installation:** Erect physical safety barriers 10 meters around high-risk pressure equipment.
3. **ESD Interlock Verification:** Verify Emergency Shutdown (ESD) pushbuttons and automated isolation valves are armed.
